Privacy and cookies

What this site collects.

This is a student project, not the official LookBerry website. It asks for no personal information. The notes below describe what the site does today.

What the site does not do

  • The contact form is a demo. It sends nothing and stores nothing.
  • There are no accounts, no newsletter and no advertising pixels (Meta, LinkedIn or Google Ads).
  • Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Measurement tools

  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 are used to count visits, see which pages and links are used and learn which campaign source a visit came from.
  • They load only after you choose Accept measurement. Before that, and if you decline, no measurement script runs and no measurement cookie is set.
  • The data is aggregated and used only to judge this student project.
  • Because the measurement ID is set in the site configuration, the tools are off until the project team switches them on.

Your choice

  • Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage) so the site does not ask again.
  • Once measurement is switched on, you can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Still to be confirmed

  • Name and contact details of the person or organisation responsible for the data
  • Legal review of this notice before any public launch
  • Retention period for analytics data, as configured in Google Analytics
