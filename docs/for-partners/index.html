Current partnership terms

  • Partners may contribute products or services to production under agreed terms.
  • Barter contributions are in-kind production support, not cash revenue.
  • Participation guarantees no reach, engagement or sales.

Paid advertising integrations are a future possibility. They have not launched, and no package or price has been set.
