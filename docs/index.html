Straight answers

Questions worth asking

Is every product shown in Fashion Battles available to buy?

No. A featured product can be bought through LookBerry where it is listed. Not every product shown is necessarily available.

How does the marketplace earn commission?

LookBerry earns a 10% commission on each marketplace sale. Listing fees are not part of the model.

What do Attraction Likes and Style Likes refer to?

Attraction Likes are for the model; Style Likes are for the look. A purchase carries more voting weight than a like alone. The strategy does not confirm a weighting ratio.

Does taking part guarantee reach, engagement or sales?

No. Participation guarantees no reach, engagement or sales.
